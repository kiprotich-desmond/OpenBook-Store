About OpenBook Store

OpenBook Store exists because ebook and research access shouldn't require choosing between overpriced marketplaces and grey-market piracy. Every title here is sourced legitimately — public domain classics, open-access research, and licensed or original work — so the platform can grow honestly, without cutting corners on where the content comes from.

We're a small, independent team building this in the open, one feature at a time.
