Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our Commitment

OpenBook Store aims to be usable by people of all abilities, in line with WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines, as our platform serves an international audience.

2. Design Choices

High-contrast text on a cream background, clear typographic hierarchy, and sharp, legible layout are chosen with readability in mind.

3. Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements — nav links, buttons, forms — are reachable and operable via standard keyboard navigation.

4. Screen Readers

Images include descriptive alt text; forms include associated labels.

5. Ongoing Work

Accessibility auditing is ongoing as the platform moves from prototype to production.

6. Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact desmondkiprotich37@gmail.com and we will address it.
