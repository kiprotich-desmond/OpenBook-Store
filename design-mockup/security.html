Security

Last updated: August 2026

1. Encrypted Connections

All traffic to OpenBook Store is served over HTTPS with an active SSL/TLS certificate, encrypting data in transit between your device and our servers.

2. Payment Security

Payment processing is handled by a PCI-compliant third-party provider; we never store full card numbers on our own servers.

3. Account Protection

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. We recommend unique passwords and enabling any available two-factor options.

4. Admin Access Controls

Administrative functions require authenticated login and are not exposed to public navigation.

5. Purchase Watermarking

Purchased files include a light embedded identifier to deter unauthorized redistribution, without affecting readability.

6. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security issue, please report it responsibly to desmondkiprotich37@gmail.com before public disclosure.
