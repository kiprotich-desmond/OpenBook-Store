1. What Cookies We Use

OpenBook Store uses browser local storage (functionally similar to cookies) to remember your cart, wishlist, and favourites between visits on the same device.

2. Essential Storage

Cart and session data is essential for the store to function and cannot be disabled without breaking checkout.

3. No Third-Party Ad Tracking

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Analytics

A future production version may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage; this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Managing Storage

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings; this will reset your cart, wishlist, and favourites.

6. International Users

Where required by regional law (e.g., EU ePrivacy rules), we will present a cookie consent banner in the production release.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as functionality changes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
