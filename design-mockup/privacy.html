Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies internationally

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenBook Store collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Platform, wherever in the world you access it from.

2. Information We Collect

Account details (name, email), purchase history, wishlist and favourites data, and any information you submit via contact or review forms.

3. Payment Information

Payment card details are processed directly by our third-party, PCI-compliant payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own servers.

4. How We Use Information

To operate your account, process purchases, deliver files, generate receipts, and respond to support requests.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (International Users)

Where applicable law requires it (such as GDPR for EU/UK users), we process personal data based on contract necessity, legitimate interest, or your consent.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your cart, wishlist, and favourites. See our separate Cookie Policy for detail.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data to third parties. Limited data is shared with payment processors solely to complete transactions.

8. Data Retention

Account and purchase data is retained as long as your account is active, or as required for tax and accounting purposes thereafter.

9. International Data Transfers

Because OpenBook Store serves an international audience, data may be processed in a country other than your own. We take reasonable steps to protect data in line with this policy regardless of location.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age applicable in their jurisdiction.

12. Email Communications

We send transactional emails (receipts, account notices) and, only with your consent, occasional updates. You may opt out of non-essential emails at any time.

13. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (SSL/TLS) and access controls to protect data. See our separate Security page for detail.

14. Third-Party Sign-In Data

If you sign in via Google or Facebook, we receive basic profile information (name, email) as permitted by your settings with that provider.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date.

16. Contact
