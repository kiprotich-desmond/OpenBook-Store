Content Licensing

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

This page explains the licensing basis for every category of content distributed through OpenBook Store.

2. Public Domain Works

Titles whose copyright has expired under the law of their country of origin, or which were never copyrighted, sourced from Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive, and HathiTrust.

3. Open-Access Research

Papers published under open licenses (commonly CC-BY or CC-BY-SA) from sources such as arXiv, PubMed Central, and DOAJ, each retaining its original attribution requirements.

4. Licensed Titles

Content distributed under a direct agreement with the rights holder. The specific terms of each license are displayed on that title's detail page.

5. Original Titles

Content created for and published exclusively through OpenBook Store under agreement with the author.

6. Attribution Requirements

Where a source license requires attribution (e.g., CC-BY), that attribution is displayed on the title's detail page and must be preserved in any permitted redistribution.

7. What Purchase Does Not Grant

Purchasing a title does not transfer copyright or grant redistribution, resale, translation, or derivative-work rights beyond what the source license or your personal reading license allows.

8. Reporting a Licensing Concern

If you believe a title is listed in error or without proper rights, contact desmondkiprotich37@gmail.com immediately and we will investigate and remove the listing if warranted.

9. Changes to Licensing Terms

Individual title licenses may be updated if the underlying source license changes. This will be reflected on the affected title's page.
